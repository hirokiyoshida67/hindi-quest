What counts toward 300?

The 300 entries include words, fixed expressions and everyday English loanwords used in Hindi. Repetitions and inflected forms are not counted as extra entries. A word counts as answered without hints when you get it right on the first try with no help. Retained means you did that on at least two different days; it is a practice milestone, not a fluency certificate.

Words you miss or need help with go into review. Each word earns XP once per day. The 31-reply conversation course is separate and does not automatically add to your vocabulary total. Use both courses: remembering a word and using it in conversation are different skills.
